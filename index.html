THE CLIENT BENEFIT

More than a home search. A smarter look at your options.

Many buyers focus on purchase price and monthly payments without knowing what assistance may be available. HomeAccessIQ brings potential opportunities into the conversation early, so you can make better-informed decisions.

01 / DISCOVER

HOW THE RESEARCH WORKS

Research guided by your homebuying situation.

During a private consultation, we discuss relevant details to help narrow the search. Depending on the program, research may consider:

Where you're buying

State, local jurisdiction, and property location requirements.

Your buyer profile

First-time or repeat buyer status, household factors, income ranges, and applicable occupation or service-related criteria.

Program rules

Potential assistance amounts, financing compatibility, credit or education requirements, repayment terms, and lender participation.

Program coverage and availability vary by location. Research is informational and does not constitute an approval, lending commitment, or guarantee of assistance.
